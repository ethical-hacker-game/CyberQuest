Precision to the tenth, at production volume

Halloway Precision has machined close-tolerance components in Rockford since 1974. We run five-axis milling, Swiss turning, and centreless grinding across two plants, supplying tier-one aerospace and automotive customers under long-term supply agreements.

Our process control is built around full traceability. Every part carries a lot record from raw bar stock through final inspection, and our process historian retains machine telemetry for the life of the programme.
