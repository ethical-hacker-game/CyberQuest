Process control and traceability

Machine telemetry, tool life, and in-process gauging data are collected continuously by our process historian and retained for the life of each programme. Lot traceability runs from certified raw stock through heat treat, finishing, and final inspection.

Historian and manufacturing-execution integration was originally commissioned by an external systems integrator. Data retention and reporting are maintained by Halloway IT Operations.

Materials
