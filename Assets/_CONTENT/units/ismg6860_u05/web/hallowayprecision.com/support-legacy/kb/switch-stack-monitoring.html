Switch stack monitoring — polling configuration

Posted 17 May 2022 by bw_tech1

Plant 2 core stack (HAL-CORE-SW, 10.30.14.1) is polled read-only from the BridgeWorks NOC collector. SNMPv2c only until the planned v3 cutover.

Excerpt from the commissioning poller notes left on the share:

! HAL-CORE-SW: BridgeWorks read-only poller
snmp-server community brdgwks-ro RO
snmp-server location Halloway Precision - Plant 2 MDF
snmp-server contact BridgeWorks Automation NOC <noc@bridgeworksautomation.com>

Do not use the vendor default community string. The custom string above is recorded in the plant runbook and must match the collector configuration.
