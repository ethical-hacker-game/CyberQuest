Quality management system

Halloway operates an AS9100D quality management system with ISO 9001:2015 certification maintained continuously since 2003. Our system covers contract review, design support, purchasing controls, production, inspection, and corrective action.

Customer source inspection and delegated inspection authority are supported at both plants. Certificates of conformance accompany every shipment.

Inspection

Final inspection is performed on two coordinate measuring machines with a 1200 × 800 mm envelope, supported by optical comparators, surface finish testing, and hardness verification. Gauge calibration is managed on a rolling twelve-month cycle against NIST-traceable standards.
