Investigation: Phishing landing capture: arcadia-support[.]com

Archived capture and sanitized evidence of a phishing landing page and related email headers linked to arcadia-support.com.

1) Executive summary Mid-Dec 2025

A look-alike domain (arcadia-support.com) was observed hosting a cloned support/login experience resembling Arcadia Robotics. An archived landing page capture and sanitized phishing email headers show messages originating from the look-alike domain, with hosting and DNS details indicating active mail capability.

This infrastructure is consistent with reconnaissance and credential collection activity and should be prioritized for monitoring and coordinated takedown requests with relevant providers.

2) Archived landing page capture (visual artifact)

Screenshot summary (sanitized)

  • Visible browser address bar: https://www.arcadia-support.com/login
  • Page header text: “Arcadia Robotics: Support Login”
  • Visible fields: corporate email input and password input, button labeled “Sign in”
  • Brand similarity: visual styling is similar to Arcadia’s public branding (color palette and logo style); the logo is visually similar but not identical.

7) Interpretation & indicators (actionable, non-technical)

  • Fresh look-alike domain matching brand + active mail server = likely phishing infrastructure.
  • Visual clone of support login is consistent with credential harvesting landing pages.
  • Email header shows messages claiming to be internal but originating from the look-alike domain and failing Arcadia’s SPF/DKIM.

These are detection and defense signals intended for monitoring and triage; this report does not include operational exploitation instructions.

8) Recommended immediate response (high-level defensive options)

  • Consider monitoring authentication, email security, and web proxy logs for indicators related to arcadia-support.com and associated infrastructure.
  • Consider issuing a short employee advisory reminding staff to verify login URLs and to report suspicious messages referencing “support account” verification.
  • Consider submitting a registrar/hosting abuse report and takedown request referencing the archived capture ID and sanitized evidence.
