DataSequence Reviews Executive Impersonation Incident

By Charisse Morgangood

June 9, 2025

DataSequence said a fraudulent social account impersonating CEO George Wilkerson published a false announcement about the company. DataSequence issued a correction, but its share price had already fallen as the claim circulated.

The false post referenced Eli Masters, whom Wilkerson has previously described in public as a longtime friend. Investigators have not established whether the impersonator learned about that relationship from prior coverage, social media, or another source.

DataSequence said it has found no evidence that its verified corporate accounts were compromised. The company asked investors and partners to confirm significant announcements through its official website and verified channels while the review continues.

The review includes the fraudulent profile, the timing and reach of its posts, and public information that could make an impersonation more convincing. DataSequence has not attributed the account to a person or group.

Wilkerson said the response should make authentic communications easier to verify without placing blame on employees or personal contacts whose information was already public.
