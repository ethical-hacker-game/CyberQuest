Security Center

Protecting your account is our priority. Learn how to spot suspicious messages and how to report them.

Common signs of phishing

  • Urgent or threatening tone that pressures you to act quickly
  • Unexpected links or attachments you were not anticipating
  • Mismatched sender details (name and address don’t align)

Safe communication basics

  • Always verify the sender domain before trusting a message
  • Never share full login credentials via email
  • If something feels off, pause and contact support using official contact details

Report suspected phishing

If you receive a message that appears to impersonate Summit Trust Bank, please report it so our team can review. See Report Phishing for the in-game reporting address and what to include.
